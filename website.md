## Tierva

![Tierva logo](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/BastionFold/Tierva/main/logo.svg)

**Status:** In development

See your land the way the satellites do, learn what to grow, and let the world protect the farmers who feed it. Underneath runs **Pacto** — a giving protocol that pays farmers the moment the satellites confirm a drought.

### What's new
- Three tiers: See the data, Understand it, then Decide what to grow
- Pacto drought vertical proven on-chain
